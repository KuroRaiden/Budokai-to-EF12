# Format references and external resources

The AMM reader and retargeting workflow were developed with reference to the public format investigations in [novapowers0/DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection](https://github.com/novapowers0/DBZ-Budokai-3-HD-Collection), including the `sb_amm.py`, `altura.py`, `b1port.py` and `sbport.py` tooling. Those upstream tools are not bundled in this repository. The included PSP skeleton reader supports the local AMO layout used by this editor.

Blender, Pillow, EF-12 creator resources, exporters, converters and their licenses belong to their respective authors. Install the required tools separately and comply with their terms. Game assets and trademarks belong to their owners and are not distributed here. Prepared meshes, rig inspection data and character templates must come from the user's local workspace.
