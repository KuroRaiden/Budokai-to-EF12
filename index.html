04 · Combo strings

Connect your moves

Create a string or branch from an earlier move. The first move keeps its VF entry command; your follow-ups replace that entry’s original combo routes. Other VF entry moves stay available. L → engine A · H → B · E → Y. E selects an attack animation; Ki effects and charging are separate.

Select a library animation.

Pick another animation in the library before adding each move. To branch, change a move’s “Follow after” to an earlier step and give it a different input.

Timing is written to EF-12’s native command and cancel fields. The mesh preview plays one move at a time. Test strings in-game to tune timing, collisions and damage.
